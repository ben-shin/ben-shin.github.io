Explainer

Four stages worth explaining

1

Soluble ensemble

Proteins diffuse as monomers or small reversible assemblies. Most contacts are unproductive.

2

Nucleation barrier

A rare arrangement survives long enough to template further assembly. This controls the lag phase.

3

Growth phase

Existing ordered assemblies recruit soluble protein, so apparent aggregation accelerates.

4

Plateau

The curve flattens as soluble material is depleted or the system reaches a new steady state.

Scientific honesty

What this page is, and what it is not

This playground is a conceptual teaching tool. It does not infer rate constants, nucleation order, off-pathway states, fragmentation rates, or microscopic mechanisms from data. To make a mechanistic claim, you would fit concentration-dependent timecourses to explicit ODE or master-equation models.
