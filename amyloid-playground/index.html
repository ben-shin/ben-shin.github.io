Experimental readouts

What would each experiment seem to say?

ThT

Clear sigmoidal aggregation

Fibril-associated signal rises after a lag phase and approaches a visible endpoint.

FCS

Slow species appear before endpoint

Diffusion shifts earlier than the final ThT plateau, suggesting soluble assemblies or growing particles.

NMR

Soluble signal decays

Peak intensity loss would report disappearance from the fast-tumbling monomeric pool.

EM

Visible fibrils late

Structures become easy to see only after enough ordered mass accumulates.

Scientific honesty

What this page can and cannot claim

This playground is an intuition tool. It does not fit microscopic rate constants, prove nucleation order, or distinguish every possible on-pathway and off-pathway mechanism. To make a mechanistic claim, pair concentration-dependent timecourses with explicit ODE or master-equation models and independent structural readouts.

M = soluble monomer, O = oligomeric intermediate, F = fibril, X = off-pathway material
M → O depends on concentration and nucleator-like activity
O → F competes with inhibitor-like suppression and off-pathway trapping
F growth increases when seed, elongation, or fragmentation-like amplification is high
