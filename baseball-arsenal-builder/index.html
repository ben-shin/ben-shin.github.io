What this tool is trying to help you answer

1. Can you throw your best pitch for a strike?

Whiff stuff only plays if you can land it early enough to avoid predictable fastball counts.

2. Is your miss pattern useful?

A slider that misses low glove side is very different from one that backs up middle-middle.

3. Do you have a count-specific plan?

The app weights pitch suggestions differently in get-ahead, neutral, leverage, and three-ball counts.

4. Are you tunneling or just mixing randomly?

The recommendation engine rewards pitch pairs with speed separation and complementary movement.
